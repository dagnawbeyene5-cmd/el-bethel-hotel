ABOUT US

A proper stay, not just a bed for the night.

El-Bethel Hotel is located in Bahir Dar, one of Ethiopia's most beautiful cities, known for Lake Tana and its calm, green streets. As a hotel near Bahir Dar Stadium, we're easy to find and close to the city's main sights. Every room is cleaned and checked before you ever see it, and our team is around from early morning to late night if you need anything at all.

  • Free Wi-Fi and hot water, all day, every room
  • 24-hour front desk and airport pickup on request
  • Restaurant serving local and international dishes
